      How to use this

      If you are already faster than a goal

      That event is ahead of the rest of your range. Enter that faster time instead and rebuild the ladder from there.

      If a goal feels far away

      Look at the gap, not the number. A 200 goal that is 6 seconds away is a season target. One that is 25 seconds away is a two season target.

      Keep one course. Use a short course best time for short course goals and a long course best time for long course goals. Do not mix them. Bring your ladder to your coach before you set a season target.
